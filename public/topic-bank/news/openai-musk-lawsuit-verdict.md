# 訴訟時效已過！馬斯克控告 OpenAI 案遭陪審團認定敗訴，法官隨即當場駁回 | Musk Loses Case Against OpenAI After Jury Finds He Waited Too Long to Sue

加州奧克蘭的一個陪審團於週一做出裁決：埃隆·馬斯克（Elon Musk）起訴 OpenAI 及其領導層的動作拖延太久。經過約 90 分鐘的審議，陪審團判定，他所提起的訴訟已超過了訴訟時效。雖然陪審團的裁決僅具諮詢性質，但聯邦法官伊芳·岡薩雷茲·羅傑斯（Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers）表示她完全同意陪審團的看法。

Elon Musk waited too long to sue OpenAI and its leaders, a jury in Oakland, California, decided on Monday. A lawsuit he filed was barred by the statute of limitations, the jury found after about 90 minutes of deliberation. Their verdict was advisory, but Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers said she agrees with the jury.

「法院現確認此前已表明之意向，即接受陪審團的認定，並將其視為本院的裁決，」羅傑斯法官表示。她於週一在法庭上補充說：「我認為有非常充分的證據支持陪審團的認定，這也是為什麼我準備當場駁回此案。」

“The court now confirms the prior indication that it would accept the jury’s findings as its own,” Rogers said. “I think that there’s a substantial amount of evidence to support the jury’s finding, which is why I was prepared to dismiss on the spot,” she added in court on Monday.

馬斯克曾協助創立並資助 OpenAI，在創立初期共投入了 3,800 萬美元。他在 2024 年 2 月起訴了執行長山姆·奧特曼（Sam Altman）、公司總裁格雷格·布羅克曼（Greg Brockman）及 OpenAI 實體，指控他們在轉向包含營利分支的架構時「竊取了慈善機構」並實現了不當得利。「我以前真是個傻瓜，」馬斯克在本月早些時候出庭時說，「我給了他們免費的資金去創辦了一家新創公司。」

Musk helped cofound and fund OpenAI, giving $38 million in its early years. He sued CEO Sam Altman, company president Greg Brockman, and OpenAI in February 2024, alleging that they “stole a charity” and unjustly enriched themselves when they shifted to a structure that includes a for-profit arm. “I was a fool,” Musk told the court earlier this month. “I gave them free funding to create a startup.”

馬斯克的官司曾威脅要阻礙這家 ChatGPT 製造商，因為該公司正計劃進行一場可能極具轟動性的 IPO。陪審團的這一裁決，對 OpenAI 以及其創辦人奧特曼和布羅克曼而言，是一次重大的勝利。

Musk’s case threatened to derail the ChatGPT maker as it plans what could be a blockbuster IPO. The jury’s decision is a win for OpenAI and its founders, Altman and Brockman.

OpenAI 的代表律師則辯稱，公司的使命並未改變，依然由一個非營利基金會的董事會管理，且馬斯克一直拖延到他創辦了自己的競爭性人工智慧公司 xAI 後才提起訴訟。陪審團對此表示贊同，認定馬斯克早在 2021 年就已經知悉訴訟中所涉及的相關行為。

OpenAI’s attorneys argued the company’s mission hasn’t changed, that it’s still run by a non-profit foundation board, and that Musk waited to file the suit until he founded his own competing artificial intelligence company, xAI. The jury agreed, finding that Musk was aware of the behavior discussed in the lawsuit as early as 2021.

「陪審團的認定證實了，這場訴訟純粹是一次企圖破壞競爭對手的虛偽嘗試，」OpenAI 的代表律師威廉·薩維特（William Savitt）在週一裁決出爐後表示。「事實是，OpenAI 是一個非營利、使命驅動的組織，過去如此，未來也將繼續忠實於這項使命。」

“The finding of the jury confirms that what this lawsuit was was a hypocritical attempt to sabotage a competitor,” William Savitt, OpenAI’s attorney, said on Monday after the verdict. “The fact is that OpenAI is a not-for-profit, mission-driven organization that has been and will continue to be faithful to that mission.”

OpenAI 的律師在審判期間還辯稱，馬斯克的捐款並未附帶公司必須保持非營利的承諾，而且這位特斯拉（Tesla）執行長在多個時間點都曾推動 OpenAI 成立營利實體以與 Google 競爭。他們還表示，馬斯克曾試圖奪取該營利實體的控制權，並在失敗後選擇離開公司。

OpenAI’s attorneys also argued during the trial that Musk’s donations did not come with a commitment to stay a nonprofit and that the Tesla CEO at various points pushed for OpenAI to create a for-profit entity to compete with Google. They also said Musk tried to gain control of the for-profit entity and left the company when he failed to do so.

馬斯克曾請求法院強迫 OpenAI 向其非營利分支退還超過 1,300 億美元的資金、免除奧特曼和布羅克曼的領導職務，並撤銷將 OpenAI 轉變為全球最具價值科技公司之一的企業重組架構。

Musk asked the court to force OpenAI to pay back more than $130 billion to OpenAI’s nonprofit arm, remove Altman and Brockman from their leadership roles and unwind the corporate restructuring that turned OpenAI into one of the world’s most valuable tech companies.

## ⚖️ 世紀審判的關鍵證詞與爆料 | Key Testimony and Revelations from the Trial

### 超級證人登台與情感糾葛

亮點 1

OpenAI 共同創辦人伊利亞·蘇茨克維（Ilya Sutskever）以及馬斯克旗下公司的執行高層、前 OpenAI 董事會成員且與馬斯克育有四個孩子的希馮·齊里斯（Shivon Zilis）等重量級證人，皆出席了本案的庭審作證。馬斯克的私人感情關係也因此成為了庭審焦點，因為齊里斯的證詞揭露了兩人的親密關係持續時間遠比外界先前所知的要長得多。法庭上還透露了在馬斯克離開 OpenAI 後，她如何作為馬斯克獲取 OpenAI 內部資訊管道的相關細節。

OpenAI cofounder Ilya Sutskever and Shivon Zilis, an executive at Musk’s companies, former OpenAI board member and mother of four of Musk’s children, were among the high-profile witnesses who testified. Musk’s romantic relationship was also in the spotlight, as Zilis’ testimony revealed the two had been romantically connected far longer than was previously known. Details about how she served as a conduit to Musk about OpenAI after his departure were also revealed in court.

### 極秘對話與內部文件曝光

亮點 2

庭審中提交了數百頁的私人電子郵件、簡訊和內部會議記錄作為證據，其中包括布羅克曼的個人日記，以及馬斯克與 Meta 執行長馬克·祖克柏（Mark Zuckerberg）之間探討是否可能聯手買下 OpenAI 的簡訊對話。這場審判還揭示了 Google 在 OpenAI 創立中所扮演的角色，當時這家 AI 實驗室正尋求超越被視為新興 AI 競賽領頭羊的對手。此外，審判還披露了 OpenAI 為了支持其龐大的運算與基礎設施需求而考慮過的眾多籌資方案，甚至包括發行加密貨幣。

Hundreds of pages worth of private emails, text messages and internal meeting notes were submitted as evidence, including Brockman’s personal diaries, and texts between Musk and Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg discussing possibly trying to buy OpenAI together. The trial revealed Google’s role in OpenAI’s founding as the AI lab sought to surpass what was then considered the leader in the nascent AI race. It also revealed the many fundraising options OpenAI considered to support its computing and infrastructure needs, including cryptocurrency.

### 微軟同列被告與馬斯克離職背景

亮點 3

微軟也因投資 OpenAI 而被指控為「共犯」並列為共同被告。馬斯克於 2018 年離開 OpenAI，並於 2023 年創辦了他自己的人工智慧公司 xAI。「本案的事實和時間軸早已非常明朗，我們對陪審團決定因逾期而駁回這些指控的裁決表示歡迎，」微軟發言人在週一陪審團做出裁決後發表聲明稱。

Microsoft was also named as a codefendant for “aiding and abetting” the claims through its investments in OpenAI. Musk left OpenAI in 2018 and started his own AI company, xAI, in 2023. “The facts and the timeline in this case have long been clear, and we welcome the jury’s decision to dismiss these claims as untimely,” a Microsoft spokesperson said in a statement after the jury’s decision on Monday.

## 💥 馬斯克強烈反彈：怒轟判決並揚言上訴 | Musk's Furious Backlash & Appeal Plans

馬斯克對此裁決極度憤怒，在其社交媒體平台 X 上發文，痛斥羅傑斯法官是一位「糟糕的積極分子」法官，並且「開創了一個極其惡劣的先例」。他於週一陪審團裁決後寫道：「她剛剛發放了一張免費許可證，只要你能把掠奪行為隱瞞個幾年，就可以隨意掠奪慈善機構！」

Musk called Rogers a “terrible activist” judge who “creates such a terrible precedent” in a post on his social media platform, X. “She just handed out a free license to loot charities if you can keep the looting quiet for a few years!” he wrote on Monday after the jury’s decision.

馬斯克的首席律師馬克·托貝羅夫（Marc Toberoff）在裁決後的記者會上表示，他們計劃提出上訴。托貝羅夫說：「這在本質上是一場鬧劇，若非馬斯克挺身而出，他們就能逍遙法外，而他們不應該得逞。」

Musk’s lead attorney, Marc Toberoff, said at a press conference after the verdict that they plan to appeal. “This at its core is a travesty, and but for Musk, they get away with it and they shouldn’t,” Toberoff said.
